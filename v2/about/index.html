About Nico Singh and Triple Down.

Triple Down is a hospitality, events and culture agency based in Leicester, founded by Nico Singh.

We work with venues, bars, hospitality groups, property developers and cultural organisations on the whole picture: what the brand stands for, how the room runs, how people find out about it, and whether it makes money at the end of the month.

Nico Singh, Founder

I've spent 14 years building brands, spaces and events across music, hospitality and independent culture.

It started with Beastwang, which I started in 2012: 180+ events and 100+ headline artists, including Chase & Status, Shy FX, Stormzy and Sammy Virji. We sold out Leicester's largest indoor show at 3,000 capacity and set the record bar spend at the O2 Academy.

Wang Records followed: millions of streams, repeat BBC Radio 1 support and sync placements in Netflix programming. Then Doppler, an independent venue and radio station that's now our HQ on the High Street. Along the way I co-founded Audrey, a cocktail bar that was booked out three months before it opened and is still trading today.

Now I'm building Daal & Bass, a South Asian music and culture brand spanning events, clothing and a record label. Its Aisle by Aisle series takes DJs and live performers into South Asian supermarkets, and it reaches 250,000 to 1 million Instagram views a month.

I've programmed a 5,000-capacity festival stage at Forbidden Forest that sold out all four editions, and I've hired and managed teams of up to 50.

Triple Down applies that experience to other people's venues and projects. I lead the strategy and stay close to every client, with a team delivering across content, marketing, programming and production.
