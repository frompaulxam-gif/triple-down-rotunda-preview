Culture-led. Commercially minded. Built to deliver.

We're Triple Down, a hospitality and events agency based in Leicester.

We work with the people behind venues, brands, festivals and cultural projects, connecting creative ideas with the structure needed to make them work.

Our experience comes from being part of the industry. We understand the decisions that shape a programme, the demands of running a room and the work that happens before anyone arrives.

Growth should strengthen what people care about.

The character of a venue or event comes from its people, ideas and community. As the business develops, that character needs to be supported by clear decisions and a workable operation.

We help bring those things together. Our work can span positioning, programming, operations, marketing and partnerships, always with an understanding of the wider business.

We take culture seriously. We take the budget, the process and the detail seriously too.

Why Triple Down?

To double down is to commit. Triple Down means going one better: taking ownership, working through the detail and seeing the job through.

That is the standard we bring to the people and projects we work with.

What that means in practice

We work with people.

We listen to the people closest to the business, respect their knowledge and bring specialists into a shared plan.

We explain the thinking.

Our recommendations have a reason behind them. We make that reasoning clear so you can assess the direction and make informed decisions.

We follow through.

We take responsibility for the work within our scope, stay close to the detail and address problems as they arise.

We think beyond the next event.

The immediate work should contribute to a business or project that can keep developing. That means considering the processes and relationships needed after launch.

The people behind the work.

We bring together experience across hospitality, music, events and communication. Our work is shaped by practical involvement in the industry and a shared commitment to doing the job properly.
