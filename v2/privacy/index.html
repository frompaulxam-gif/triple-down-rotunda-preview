Privacy

How this review website handles information.

Contacting Triple Down

Use systems@tripledown.group to contact us about an enquiry or ask about information you have shared. When you send the enquiry form, your name, email address, project message and any optional details are processed through a Cloudflare Worker and delivered through SMTP2GO to our enquiry inbox. These details are used to respond to the enquiry, not to subscribe you to marketing.

Website navigation

The website uses session storage to remember your gallery position and whether you have chosen to pause its movement. This stays in your browser and is not sent as an enquiry.

Analytics and hosting

No optional analytics or advertising tools have been added to this website. The public preview is hosted by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information to serve and protect the website.

Before the agency website launches

The responsible legal entity and final form delivery arrangements need to be confirmed before this notice is finalised.
