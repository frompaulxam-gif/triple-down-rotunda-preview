Moonshine Speakeasy · Websites / Copywriting

Moonshine: a website that feels like the venue

Moonshine has a distinct character. Cocktails, live music and late nights in an intimate Leicester setting. Its website needed to give people a sense of that experience before they arrived.

We designed and built a bespoke website for the venue, with a complete design overhaul and a full rewrite of the copy. The brief was to create a more premium, on-brand presence, make the customer journey easier to navigate and support its visibility in search.

Bespoke website design and build; full copy rewrite; customer journey; SEO objectives.

The guest experience starts online

For a hospitality business, the website is part of the welcome. It helps people decide whether a venue feels right for them, what kind of visit to plan and what they need to know before booking.

That matters at Moonshine, where guests can arrive with quite different plans. Someone coming for a live performance needs different information from a group arranging drinks or a person enquiring about private hire. Each should be able to recognise the experience they are looking for.

Bringing the design and words together

The project covered both the design and the copy, so we could develop them as one connected experience.

The design needed to carry Moonshine's character through the site, giving visitors a consistent sense of the place as they explored its different offers.

The rewrite had an equally practical job. The language needed to carry the atmosphere, explain the different offers and make the next step clear. An introduction to the speakeasy, an event listing and a private-hire enquiry all serve different purposes, but they should still feel like the same business speaking.

That balance shaped the copy: enough personality to reflect Moonshine, with the clarity people need to plan a visit.

Helping people find their kind of night

The new site brings together the programme, table bookings, brunch and private hire, with dedicated routes for the different ways people use the venue.

The What's On page lets visitors explore events by category and search by artist or genre. The table-booking page explains the different areas of Moonshine, helping guests understand their options before choosing where to sit. Private hire has its own information and enquiry route for people planning an occasion.

Those details connect the appeal of the venue with the decisions a guest needs to make. The website can introduce the experience and help someone take the next step, whether that is choosing a performance, arranging a table or discussing an event.

Giving search a place in the brief

Improving SEO was an objective alongside the design and customer journey. The content needed to describe the venue and its offer clearly, with Moonshine's Leicester location and different experiences easy to understand, while keeping its own tone of voice.

The completed project gives Moonshine a bespoke website with a fully reworked design and rewritten copy. It brings the venue's identity and practical visitor information together in a more considered online experience.
