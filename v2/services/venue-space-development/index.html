Venue & Space Development

Make the space work for the experience.

A venue needs to work for the people visiting and the team running it. We connect the concept with the layout, processes and practical requirements behind the operation.

We can help shape a new use for a space, improve an established venue or organise the work required for its next stage.

Understand how the venue needs to function.

You may have a promising space without a clear operating plan, a venue that is difficult to run consistently or an opportunity to introduce a new offer.

We look at how people move through the space, how the team works and what the concept needs to deliver. The recommendations should make sense commercially and in day-to-day use.

What we work on

Space development and process design

Exploring how a space can support the offer, then mapping the processes around it. This can include arrival, service, booking journeys and the way different uses fit together.

Venue management

Coordinating the people, processes and day-to-day priorities behind the venue's operation. We establish the responsibilities, reporting and working relationship with the venue team before taking on delivery.

Operational structure

Clarifying who is responsible for what, how decisions are made and how the team prepares, delivers and reviews its work.

Infrastructure planning

Identifying the practical requirements behind the concept and coordinating the relevant specialists where the project calls for them.

A venue the team can run with confidence.

Depending on the brief, the work may include an operating framework, process maps, a phased development plan or a clearer structure for management and reporting.

We connect these decisions with the programme, positioning and customer offer so the venue works as one business.
