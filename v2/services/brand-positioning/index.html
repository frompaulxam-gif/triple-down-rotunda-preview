Brand & Positioning

Be clear about what makes people choose you.

A strong brand gives people a clear sense of what to expect and why it matters to them. We help hospitality businesses and cultural projects define that position and express it consistently.

The work connects the idea behind the business with its audience, communication and real-world experience.

Give the business a clear direction.

You might be developing a new concept, reaching a different audience or finding that the way you present the business no longer reflects what it has become.

We look at the offer, its context and the people it needs to reach. That creates a basis for decisions about identity, language, creative work and the experience itself.

What we work on

Brand strategy and alignment

Clarifying the proposition, audience and principles behind the brand, then checking how they connect with the business and its ambitions.

Creative direction

Setting a coherent direction for the way the brand looks and communicates, and guiding the people creating its assets.

Cultural identity

Understanding the references, communities and values that give a project its character, so its expression has a credible connection to the people behind it.

Market positioning

Establishing where the offer sits, how it differs from alternatives and what gives the intended audience a reason to care.

Give the content something clear to say.

Positioning should guide the subjects you talk about, the language you use and the experience your imagery communicates. We turn that direction into useful guidance for the people producing content.

For content planning, campaigns and ongoing delivery, explore Growth & Revenue Strategy.

A direction people can use.

The agreed work may include a positioning statement, audience priorities, a messaging framework or a creative brief. The purpose is to give your team a clear basis for decisions across the business.

Do we need a complete rebrand?

That depends on the problem. We look at what is already working and identify what needs to change before recommending the extent of the work.

Can you work with our designer or existing agency?

Yes. We can establish the strategy and creative direction alongside the people responsible for delivery, with clear roles and a shared brief.
