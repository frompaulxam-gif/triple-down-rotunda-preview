Community & Expansion

Build the relationships that support what comes next.

Hospitality and culture depend on people choosing to take part. We help businesses and projects understand their audiences, build relevant partnerships and plan their next stage of growth.

The work starts with the character of the project and the relationships that already give it value.

Grow with a clear understanding of who it is for.

You may want to reach a new audience, develop a partnership or take an established concept into another setting. Each brings questions about relevance, resources and how the experience will change.

We help work through those questions before activity expands, so the plan reflects the people, place and purpose involved.

What we work on

Audience development

Understanding who is already involved, who could be and what might help them take part. We connect those insights with the offer, programme and communication.

Strategic partnerships

Identifying organisations and collaborators with a useful shared purpose. We help shape the proposition, responsibilities and practical value of working together.

Cultural strategy

Giving a project direction that reflects its context, communities and ambitions. We connect that purpose with the programme and the work needed to deliver it.

Market expansion

Assessing a new audience, location or format, then setting out the considerations and steps needed to develop the opportunity responsibly.

A reason to grow and a plan for doing it.

The agreed work may include audience priorities, a partnership proposition, a cultural programme framework or a phased expansion plan.

We look at what the opportunity asks of the business as well as what it could offer. Programming, operations and commercial planning need to support the direction.
