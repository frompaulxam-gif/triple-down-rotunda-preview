Programming & Experience

Give people a reason to be there.

A strong programme understands its audience and the place it belongs in. We develop concepts, book artists and manage events with a clear purpose behind each decision.

Whether you're shaping a regular venue programme, planning a festival or creating a new experience, we connect the creative idea with the practical work required to deliver it.

A programme with a clear role in the business.

You might need a new reason for people to visit, more consistency across the calendar or a delivery team for a defined event. We start by understanding the audience, the setting, the budget and what success needs to look like.

That gives the programme a direction and makes individual decisions easier to assess.

What we work on

Event production and management

Turning the concept into a delivery plan, with schedules, supplier coordination, production planning and event management. We establish the responsibilities and the work we will manage before delivery begins.

Artist bookings and programming

Building line-ups and calendars around the audience, format and budget. We consider how artists and events fit together, what each booking contributes and how the programme develops over time.

Concept development

Giving an idea a clear format, audience and purpose. We explore what makes it relevant, where it fits in the offer and what it needs to be viable.

Experience design

Looking at the visit from the audience's perspective. Arrival, movement through the space, atmosphere, service and the moments people remember all shape the experience.

A shared plan for the people delivering it.

The agreed work may result in a programming framework, an event concept, a booking plan or a production schedule. We make the decisions and responsibilities clear so the people involved can work towards the same outcome.

Where the brief also needs marketing, sales planning or changes to the venue operation, we connect that work from the start.
