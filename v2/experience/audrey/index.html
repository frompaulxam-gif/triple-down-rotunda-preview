Bar I co-founded · Leicester · Opened 2021

Audrey: concept, build and launch, 2021.

Audrey is a café, restaurant and cocktail bar I co-founded in Leicester in 2021, as the city came out of lockdown. We took it from an empty space through the lease, the build, the brand, the team and the launch, then sold it. It's still trading today.

The numbers

  • Largest Instagram following of any cocktail brand in Leicester before opening
  • Weekend tables booked three months ahead of launch
  • Still trading five years on

What it took

The build
Negotiating the lease, managing contracts and budget, finding trades at short notice and running a three-month build to deadline.
The brand
An identity built to last and to scale, designed with the potential to open in other cities.
The launch
We built the audience before the doors opened, using tone of voice and creative to reach exactly the crowd we wanted. By launch, weekends were booked out three months ahead.
The team
We hired an experienced bar manager, who trained a team with no previous bar experience.
