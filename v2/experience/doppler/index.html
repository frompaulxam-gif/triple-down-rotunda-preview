Doppler

An independent underground music venue and community radio station in Leicester. A space for sound, expression and connection.

Venue & community · Venue development · Music programming · Brand & marketing

Doppler Studio & Radio is an independent underground music venue, online radio station and creative space based in Leicester.

Built around community, music and DIY culture, Doppler was created as a home for underground artists, DJs, promoters, broadcasters and audiences who needed a space outside of traditional nightlife structures.

The project brings several moving parts under one roof: a live events venue, a radio platform, a recording and content space, and a community-led cultural hub. It exists as both a physical space and a wider platform for people creating, sharing and experiencing underground music.

On a day-to-day level, Doppler requires hands-on operational management across venue readiness, cleaning, maintenance, staffing, calendar management, event set-up, pack-down planning, supplier coordination and customer experience. The space has to be prepared for radio shows, private hires, club nights, community sessions, content shoots and live events, often with different needs, audiences and technical requirements.

The radio side of Doppler adds another layer to the project. It involves programming shows, onboarding residents, managing schedules, supporting DJs and broadcasters, maintaining equipment, developing content, building community engagement and creating a platform for emerging underground talent.

Behind the scenes, the project involves responsibility for event planning, talent booking, production management, hospitality, branding, marketing, social media, CRM, data management, budget control, cashflow, ticketing, bar sales, expense monitoring, licensing, contracts, insurance, health and safety, staffing, security and audience management.

Doppler also requires ongoing relationship management: working with promoters, DJs, residents, suppliers, freelancers, local creatives, community groups, customers, neighbours and wider industry contacts. Its success depends on balancing creative freedom with the structure needed to keep the space safe, organised and financially sustainable.

The project also involves practical venue considerations such as waste management, energy use, equipment maintenance, audience experience, trend monitoring and long-term community building.

Doppler Studio & Radio is a real-world example of independent music infrastructure: a space where underground culture can happen, communities can grow, and emerging talent can be supported with care, consistency and structure.
