Merchants Yard: launch and first season, 2026.

Merchants Yard opened in May 2026 as a 600-capacity courtyard venue in the centre of Leicester. We were brought in before launch to shape what it would be, who it was for and how it would run.

The numbers

  • 10,000+ guests across the first summer
  • 250k–1M Instagram views every month
  • 50+ events programmed in 2026 (twice weekly May–September, then every Saturday, bank holidays and football screenings)

What we did

Concept and programming
We built the summer around repeatable formats people could plan their week around: Sunset Yard Sessions on Fridays, Country in the Courtyard on bank holiday Sundays, World Cup screenings, brass bands and DJs, with a mix of ticketed and walk-in events.
Brand and storytelling
We created the way the Yard talks and looks, from the launch campaign to weekly programme announcements, photography, reels and email.
Operations, on the day
We're on site on event days, book the bar and door staff, and give the venue direct feedback on what's working and what needs to change, then help put it right.
Into winter
The Winter Tipi extends the season, using the same approach to keep the courtyard trading through the colder months.
